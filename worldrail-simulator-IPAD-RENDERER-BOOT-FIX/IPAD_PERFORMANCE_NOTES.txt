WorldRail iPad Performance Build
Target: 30-40 FPS on 2021-class iPad hardware.

Changes:
- 520-object library remains bundled but is no longer parsed/loaded wholesale during Drive.
- world streaming: 240 m tiles, 6 ahead / 2 behind; old tiles disposed.
- sleepers merged into one mesh per tile.
- buildings/roads merged by material.
- vegetation uses Babylon thin instances.
- no realtime shadows.
- 620 m fog/render horizon.
- antialiasing disabled at engine level.
- dynamic hardware scaling targets 30-40 FPS.
- detailed player train hidden in cab view and enabled only for chase view.
- full 520-object GLB remains available to route/editor/catalog systems.
