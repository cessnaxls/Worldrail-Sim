WORLDRAIL DRIVE V1
- Drive button now opens a live canvas train simulator.
- Touch power and brake levers.
- Train acceleration/braking with drag and overspeed indication.
- Speed limits by route segment.
- Working block signals reacting to AI trains.
- Three AI trains move independently ahead.
- Station distances and accelerated timetable clock.
- Cab/chase/trackside camera modes.
- Horn and door chime use bundled audio.
- Door interlock only allows doors at near-zero speed.
- Emergency brake.
- iPad touch/pointer controls and DPR capped at 2 for performance.
This is the first functional driving layer. It uses a lightweight 2.5D renderer so it boots reliably on iPad Safari; realistic GLB/PBR scene integration is the next graphics layer.
