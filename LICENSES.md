# WorldRail built-in realistic asset library

WorldRail does not generate primitive scenery geometry. The built-in catalog is limited to third-party artist-created assets with redistribution-compatible licenses.

- **Ordinary House** — Drummyfish — CC0 — https://opengameart.org/content/ordinary-house
- **Modular House - 3D Models** — Keith / Fertile Soil Productions — CC0 — https://opengameart.org/content/modular-house-3d-models
- **Locomotive** — 32kda — CC0 — https://opengameart.org/content/locomotive-1
- **BRSigs_Open** — Anthony Bowden, Steve Green, Tony Haylor, Simon Gathercole — public-domain subset of the OpenBVE British signalling package — https://openbve-project.net/download-extras/
- **Poly Haven** — CC0 — approved source for future realistic texture/model expansion; assets are not silently claimed as bundled unless actually loaded. https://polyhaven.com/license

WorldRail must preserve source/license metadata in exported route credits.
